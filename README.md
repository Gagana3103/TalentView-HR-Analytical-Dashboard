# TalentView-HR-Analytical-Dashboard
An interactive Power BI HR Analytics Dashboard that analyzes workforce demographics, employee attrition, and hiring trends to support data-driven HR decisions, improve retention, and optimize workforce planning.

📊 Project Overview :

This project is an HR Analytics Dashboard built using Power BI to analyze workforce composition, employee attrition, compensation trends, and new hire patterns. The dashboard helps HR teams and business leaders make data-driven decisions related to  employee retention, workforce planning, and recruitment strategy.
The report is divided into three main analytical sections:
HR Workforce Overview.
Employee Attrition Insights.
New Hire Insights.

Purpose of the Project: To transform raw HR data into actionable insights by identifying attrition drivers, workforce demographics, and hiring trends, enabling organizations to improve retention and optimize talent management strategies.

🎯 Business Objectives :

Monitor overall workforce health using key HR metrics. Identify attrition drivers across departments, roles, and tenure. Reduce voluntary attrition through targeted retention strategies. Analyze the impact of compensation and hikes on employee turnover. Evaluate hiring trends and effectiveness across departments. Support data-driven workforce planning and HR decision-making.

🛠 Tools & Technologies :

Power BI Desktop – Data modeling and visualization.

DAX – Measures and KPIs.

Power Query – Data cleaning and transformation.

Excel / CSV – Data source.

📌 Data Used :

The dataset represents an organization’s HR records and includes:

Employee demographics (age, gender, marital status, education).

Employment details (department, job role, tenure).

Compensation data (monthly income, hike percentage).

Attrition details (status, reason, year of leaving).

Hiring details (joining year, age group, education).

Geographic information (province).
The data spans multiple years, enabling trend and comparative analysis.

📊 Project Highlights :

Developed an interactive HR Analytics Dashboard using Power BI. Analyzed workforce composition, attrition, and hiring trends in a single solution. Identified high-attrition departments, roles, and tenure groups. Distinguished between voluntary and involuntary attrition with reason analysis. Evaluated the relationship between compensation, hikes, and attrition. Tracked new hire trends by year, department, age group, and education. Implemented key KPIs using DAX measures for clear performance tracking. Enabled data-driven HR decision-making through dynamic filters and visuals.

📌 Key Business Insights :

1. The organization has a young and mid-career workforce, with most employees in the 26–35 age group.

2.  Voluntary attrition is significantly higher than involuntary attrition.

3. Certain departments and roles experience consistently higher attrition.

4. Corporate Functions and Sales-related roles experience the highest attrition, making them high-risk areas.

5. Employees with shorter tenure (2–3 years) are more likely to leave.

6. Salary alone does not fully explain attrition—non-monetary factors matter.

7. Key reasons for attrition include better compensation opportunities, long working hours, medical issues, relocation, and retirement.

8. Hiring has increased steadily in recent years, especially in Product and Sales functions.

9. Product and Sales departments account for the highest number of new hires.

10. Most new hires also fall within the 26–35 age group, aligning with existing workforce demographics.
